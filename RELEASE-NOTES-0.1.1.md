# TV Home Universal 0.1.1 Preview

- Bundled PS5, PS2 System, Switch, Xbox Series, Persona 5, Pip-Boy and Portal 2 sound packs.
- Restored pack selection, effect toggle and volume slider. PS2 System is the default.
- Uses the chosen pack for navigation, app launch, menus, toggles and supported sleep/wake events.
- Loads only the selected pack; retains the wake focus sound guard.
- Includes audio source provenance and third-party notices.

This is a development-signed preview targeting Android TV 8.0+. Physical TV testing remains pending. Sleep audio may be interrupted by firmware. No TV installation was performed. Third-party recordings are excluded from the MIT code license.
